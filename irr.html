Method: Decisions are binarized as Forward (include or topical) vs. Out (any exclude variant). Only records where both reviewers in a pair have reached a complete decision are included in each pair's count. Cohen's κ is computed as (Po − Pe) / (1 − Pe). Strength labels follow Landis & Koch (1977): <0 Poor · 0.00–0.20 Slight · 0.21–0.40 Fair · 0.41–0.60 Moderate · 0.61–0.80 Substantial · 0.81–1.00 Almost perfect.
